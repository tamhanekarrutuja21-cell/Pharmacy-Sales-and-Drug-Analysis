# Pharmacy Sales & Drug Analysis

## Project overview
This Excel project analyzes a fictional pharmacy sales dataset to identify medicine usage trends and revenue patterns. It evaluates top-selling medicines, monthly sales growth, therapeutic category performance, and branded-versus-generic sales.

## Workbook contents
- **Dashboard:** KPIs, key findings, and charts.
- **Sales Data:** transaction-level records for January–December 2025.
- **Medicine Analysis:** medicine-wise units sold, revenue, and transaction counts.
- **Monthly Trends:** monthly units, revenue, and month-on-month revenue growth.
- **Branded vs Generic:** comparison by units, revenue, transactions, and revenue share.
- **Category Analysis:** therapeutic category performance.
- **Notes & Methodology:** formulas, assumptions, and limitations.

## Key formulas
- Revenue = Units Sold × Unit Price.
- Monthly revenue growth = ((Current Month Revenue − Previous Month Revenue) / Previous Month Revenue) × 100.
- January growth is blank because the previous month is not included.

## Important note
All data are synthetic and created only for educational purposes. They do not represent actual pharmacy sales, patients, prescriptions, or market research. Findings should not be used for clinical or commercial decisions.
